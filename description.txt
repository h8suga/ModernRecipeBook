Modern Recipe Book 1.5.2

A recipe book for every item in the game and its mods, in English and Russian. Open it with Mouse3 (rebindable in Controls).

Tabs: Items, Recipes, Storage (with Magic Storage), Recent crafts and Tracking. Every tab has search, a mod filter, sorting, favorites and categories, and keeps them until you change them. One stage filter in the header — Pre-Hardmode, Hardmode, Post-Moon Lord — shows the earliest point each item can be obtained: through recipe chains, vanilla and Calamity bosses and bags, shops, crates, enemy drops, banners and world generation, for your world's difficulty (Classic, Expert, Master, Calamity Revengeance and Death).

The item card shows the recipe with have/need counts, batches (×1, ×5, ×10, Max), Craft, Track and Favorite, or switches to Used in: everything the item is crafted into. Click an ingredient to open it; Back and Forward (Mouse4/Mouse5, Alt+Left/Right) walk through everything you opened. How to Get lists every source of an item, and enemy cards show what a mob drops.

Tracking: follow up to five goals (or full armor sets) on screen, as compact lines under the minimap or as cards with progress bars. The tracker never crafts or reserves items.

Press F1 in the book for all shortcuts.

Terraria 1.4.4.9 / tModLoader 2026.8.3.0. Optional Magic Storage 0.7.0.11 support.
