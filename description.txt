Modern Recipe Book 1.17.1

A recipe book and bestiary for every item and mob in the game and its mods, in English and Russian, and translatable into any language like Calamity (Localization/<culture> files). Open it with Mouse3 (rebindable in Controls).

Tabs: Items, Recipes, Bestiary, Storage (nearby chests and Magic Storage), Recent crafts and Tracking. Every tab has search (names and the item's own text), a mod filter, sorting (by damage, defense, healing, tool power and more where the category has them), favorites and categories, and keeps them until you change them. Items and Recipes can show only what you can craft right now. On Items and Recipes, untick the crafting stations you lack to hide what only they make, or sort by station from the weakest to the strongest. A stage filter in the header (Pre-Hardmode, Hardmode, Post-Moon Lord, or "Before: <boss>" for any vanilla or Calamity boss, "Next boss" by default; Storage keeps its own) shows the earliest point each item or mob can be met: through recipe chains, vanilla and Calamity bosses and bags, shops, crates, enemy drops, banners and world generation, for your world's difficulty (Classic, Expert, Master, Calamity Revengeance and Death). Class buttons (Melee, Ranged, Magic, Summoner, Rogue) narrow weapons, armor and accessories to one class; armor and accessories are classified by what they actually boost. A Traits filter narrows weapons to homing (from their own description), piercing, through walls, auto attack, minion or sentry.

A footer chip shows the crafting stations in reach (nearby, in a Magic Storage Crafting Interface, even one opened remotely, and water, snow and the like); hover it for their icons. The item card shows the recipe with have/need counts, batches (x1, x5, x10, Max), Craft, Track and Favorite, or switches to Used in: everything the item is crafted into. Click an ingredient to open it; Back and Forward (Mouse4/Mouse5, Alt+Left/Right) walk through everything you opened. How to Get lists every source of an item: recipes, drops, bags and crates, shops, and the world itself (where it grows, which blocks drop it, where to mine it, fishing, shimmer, the bug net), with the drop chances of your world's difficulty and seed. Hover any row that stands for an item to read that item's own tooltip. Recipes that differ in one ingredient show as one, "A or B"; armor cards show the set bonus.

Bestiary: every mob by kind, event and biome (Calamity's biomes included), with an animated portrait over its biome, its own drops, where and after which boss it spawns (read from the mods' own spawn code too) and, once you have met it like the vanilla Bestiary requires, its life, defense, damage, coins, kills and banner progress. Press Mouse3 on a mob in the world to open its entry. The book remembers when you first and last met each mob: "Recently seen" lists the last ones, and the Bestiary can be sorted by when you met them.

Nearby storage: chests, banks and every Magic Storage network around you (10–50 tiles, default 30) are one sorted, searchable list, with how many of their cells are taken. A bound Portable Access in your inventory brings its far network in too, without opening Magic Storage, within Magic Storage's own range rules. Take items like from a chest (click, hold right-click, Shift+Click), put them away with Shift+Click or by dropping them on the list, and craft straight from them: the book counts everything nearby and pulls the materials it needs. Works in multiplayer, where the server moves the items.

Map: Mouse3 on an explored spot of the full map teleports you there, and shared markers with a badge and an optional name show everyone who placed them and when. The host can turn nearby storage, map teleport and markers off in the server settings.

Tracking: follow up to five goals (or full armor sets) on screen, as compact lines under the minimap or as cards with progress bars. The tracker never crafts or reserves items.

Optional Double-Tap Dash (Calamity): with Calamity's Dash Double Tap Override key bound, double-tapping left/right dashes again, and the key keeps working. Off by default.

Press F1 in the book for all shortcuts.

Terraria 1.4.4.9 / tModLoader 2026.8.3.0. Optional Magic Storage 0.7.0.11 support.
