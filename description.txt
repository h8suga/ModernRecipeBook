Modern Recipe Book 1.4.8

Mouse3 searches hovered items in native inventory/container/equipment/accessory/vanity/shop/guide/reforge/material slots. Mod-window tooltip fallback and book/tracker item icons are supported. No items are moved. Mouse4/Mouse5 navigate chain history; bindings can be changed in Controls.

Compact draggable craft tracker: up to 5 independent goals. Click a goal to expand its materials. Configurable size (60-150%) and opacity (20-100%), per-character saved position, per-goal remove, one-shot readiness sound followed by fade-out. Full armor sets can be tracked as one goal, with aggregated material requirements. Tracker never auto-crafts or reserves items. Readiness means materials collected, not station/world-condition availability.

Pre-hardmode/hardmode/post-Moon-Lord filter: the earliest point each item can be obtained, through recipe chains, vanilla and Calamity boss drops and bags, town shops, crates, enemy drops and banners. The world's difficulty (Classic/Expert/Master, Calamity Revengeance) decides which drops exist. Items without a known source are estimated from rarity.

Russian/English recipe book: items, recipes, chains, optional Magic Storage, recent crafts, favorites, batch selection and material have/need counts. Armor set selector uses a stable title baseline. Native crafting APIs own consumption and outputs. Opening remains deferred to UpdateUI, outside gravity/draw hooks; duplicate-name crash fix preserved.

Terraria 1.4.4.9 / tModLoader 2026.8.3.0. Optional Magic Storage 0.7.0.11 support.
