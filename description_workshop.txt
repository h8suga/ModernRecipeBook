[h1]Modern Recipe Book[/h1]
[b]Version 1.18.2[/b] · tModLoader 2026.08 · Calamity 2.2.4 · Magic Storage 0.7.0.11 · [url=https://github.com/h8suga/ModernRecipeBook/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md]what changed[/url]

A recipe book, bestiary and storage browser for every item and mob in Terraria and its mods. Press [b]Mouse3[/b] and the book opens over the game. Everything is read from the game and its mods, not from a wiki.

[h2]New in 1.18[/h2]

Every row has Craft, Take, Track or Open one click away.
[*][b]Everything is a link[/b]: every boss, mob, biome, item, mod and stage the book names opens where it lives; Back returns.
[*][b]Contents[/b] of Treasure Bags, crates, presents and geodes; [b]chests[/b] in How to Get (299 items); drop conditions in plain words.
[*]Coins go to the Piggy Bank first, right stages for crafted items, multiplayer storage fixes, Recent counts what you crafted.

[h2]Everything it does[/h2]
[b]Six tabs[/b]: Items (every item with its card), Recipes, Bestiary, Storage, Recent and Tracking.

[b]Search and filters[/b]: by name, ID, mod or the item's own text; a mod filter; categories with icons; favorites; sorts by damage, defense, healing, tool power, flight time, rarity, price and crafting station; class buttons (melee, ranged, magic, summoner, rogue); weapon traits (homing, piercing, through walls, auto attack); "Craftable now"; untick the stations you lack. Every filter stays until you change it.

[b]Progression[/b]: every item, recipe and mob has the boss it waits for. "Before: <boss>" shows what you can get right now, "Next boss" follows your world, and the hint says why ("Spectre Bar ← Ectoplasm ← Dungeon Spirit"). Difficulty (Classic, Expert, Master, Revengeance, Death) and seed decide which drops exist.

[b]Item card[/b]: the recipe with have/need counted from everything nearby, x1/x5/x10/Max, Craft pulls the materials by itself, Track and Favorite; Used in; Contents; recipes that differ in one ingredient as "A or B"; the armor set bonus; its category, clickable.

[b]How to Get[/b]: recipes, drops with your world's chances and conditions, bags, crates, chests, shops, fishing, shimmer, mining, trees, plants, world generation and the bug net.

[b]Storage[/b]: chests, banks and Magic Storage networks within 10–50 tiles are one sorted list. Take like from a chest, Deposit All, Quick Stack and Restock, craft straight from it; a Portable Access brings a far network in.

[b]Bestiary[/b]: every mob by kind, event and biome, with an animated portrait, its own drops, where and after which boss it spawns, its stats once met and "Recently seen". Mouse3 on a mob in the world opens it.

[b]Tracking[/b]: up to five goals or armor sets on screen, as cards or compact lines under the minimap.

[b]Map[/b]: Mouse3 on the full map teleports to an explored spot; shared markers everyone sees.

[b]Keys[/b]: every action has a shortcut (F1 lists them), Back and Forward (Mouse4/5) walk through everything you opened, and hovering any item row shows its own tooltip.

[b]Magic Storage and Calamity built in[/b]: every network is part of Storage with its Crafting Interface stations; every Calamity boss is a step (no Boss Checklist needed), Calamity's biomes, Acid Rain, spawn rules and chest loot come from its own code, and Rogue has its button.

[b]Multiplayer[/b]: Host & Play and servers; the server moves the items, so nothing is duplicated or lost.

[h2]Compatibility and languages[/h2]
Terraria 1.4.4.9, tModLoader 2026.08.3.0. Calamity 2.2.4 and Magic Storage 0.7.0.11 are optional and fully supported. Needed on the server and every client; joining players download it. English and Russian; translatable like Calamity ([url=https://github.com/h8suga/ModernRecipeBook/blob/main/TRANSLATING.md]TRANSLATING.md[/url]).
