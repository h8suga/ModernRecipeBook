[h1]Modern Recipe Book[/h1]
A recipe book, bestiary and storage browser for every item and mob in Terraria and its mods. Press [b]Mouse3[/b] (middle click) and the book opens over the game. Everything in it is read from the game and its mods, not from a wiki: recipes, drops, bags, shops, crates, fishing, world generation and spawn rules.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/h8suga/ModernRecipeBook/main/docs/screenshots/items.png[/img]

[h2]Highlights[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Six tabs in one window[/b]: Items, Recipes, Bestiary, Storage, Recent crafts and Tracking.
[*][b]How to Get[/b] for any item: recipes, drops, bags, crates, shops, fishing, shimmer and the world itself, with the drop chances of your world's difficulty and seed.
[*][b]It knows where you are in the game[/b]: every item, recipe and mob has the boss it waits for, and "Before: <boss>" shows exactly what you can get right now. "Next boss" follows your world by itself.
[*][b]Craft from everything around you[/b]: inventory, chests, banks and Magic Storage, even a network far away.
[*][b]Bestiary[/b] with animated portraits, drops, where and after which boss a mob spawns, and the stats you have earned.
[*]Search by name or by the item's own text, sorts by damage, defense, healing, tool power and more, class buttons and weapon traits (homing, piercing, through walls...), a crafting station filter and "Craftable now".
[*]Hover any row that stands for an item to read its own tooltip.
[*]Tracking cards or compact lines under the minimap, map teleport and shared map markers.
[*]Every action has a shortcut; F1 lists them all. Back and Forward walk through everything you opened.
[/list]

[h2]Built-in Magic Storage and Calamity support[/h2]
[b]Magic Storage[/b]: every network in reach is part of the Storage tab, with its cells and its Crafting Interface stations. Craft straight from it, bring a far network in with a Portable Access, and Quick Stack, Deposit All and Restock into it, the remote one included.

[b]Calamity[/b]: every Calamity boss is a progression step (no Boss Checklist needed), Calamity's biomes and the Acid Rain tiers sort the Bestiary, spawn rules are read from Calamity's own code, drop chances follow Revengeance, boss-kill ores say which boss spawns them, and the Rogue class has its own button.

The mod is needed on the server and on every client; players joining a server download it. Without Calamity or Magic Storage it works with whatever the game has.

[h2]Languages[/h2]
English and Russian, including everything the book writes about items, sources and mobs. Every text lives in localization files as a whole phrase, so the book can be translated into any language the way Calamity is: see [url=https://github.com/h8suga/ModernRecipeBook/blob/main/TRANSLATING.md]TRANSLATING.md[/url].

[h2]На русском[/h2]
Книга рецептов, бестиарий и обзор хранилищ для каждого предмета и моба Terraria и её модов, полностью на русском. Открывается на колёсико мыши (Mouse3). Показывает, где взять любой предмет и после какого босса он доступен, крафтит из сундуков и Magic Storage рядом (даже из удалённой сети), полностью поддерживает Calamity и Magic Storage и работает в мультиплеере. Подробности и скриншоты — на [url=https://github.com/h8suga/ModernRecipeBook/blob/main/README.ru.md]GitHub[/url].
