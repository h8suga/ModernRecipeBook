[h1]Modern Recipe Book[/h1]
[b]Version 1.18.0[/b] · tModLoader 2026.08 · Calamity 2.2.4 · Magic Storage 0.7.0.11 · [url=https://github.com/h8suga/ModernRecipeBook/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md]what changed[/url]

A recipe book, bestiary and storage browser for every item and mob in Terraria and its mods. Press [b]Mouse3[/b] (middle click) and the book opens over the game. Everything in it is read from the game and its mods, not from a wiki: recipes, drops, bags, shops, crates, chests, fishing, world generation and spawn rules.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/h8suga/ModernRecipeBook/main/docs/screenshots/items.png[/img]

[h2]New in 1.18[/h2]
[h3]Ctrl+F searches everything[/h3]
From anywhere in the game, with the book open or not: items, storage, recipes, the Bestiary, bosses, tracked goals, categories and mods in one window.
[list]
[*]By name in any language, by what a thing is ("buff", "wings", the buff it gives, a class) and by item descriptions.
[*]Every row has its action one click away: [b]Craft[/b], [b]Take[/b] from storage, [b]Track[/b] or [b]Open[/b], with the card's keys (Ctrl+Enter, Alt+T, Alt+G...).
[*]Under a match: who drops it, which bag holds it, what a boss drops. ">" lists the book's commands.
[/list]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/h8suga/ModernRecipeBook/main/docs/screenshots/quick-search.png[/img]

[h3]Everything is a link[/h3]
Every boss, mob, biome, event, item, mod, stage and goal the book names opens where it lives, and Back returns: the bosses in loot conditions, a mob's stage and biome, How to Get's sellers, rods and pickaxes, recipe conditions, the tracker, the Storage tab's banks and networks.
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/h8suga/ModernRecipeBook/main/docs/screenshots/links.png[/img]

[h3]What a bag holds, where the world hides it[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Contents[/b]: Treasure Bags, crates, presents and geodes list what they hold, with your world's chances.
[*]How to Get knows the chests world generation fills, vanilla's and Calamity's: 299 items show the chest, chance and stack.
[*]Drop conditions in plain words: "After Astrum Aureus · not met yet", "Only the first kill · already past". No more "Special condition" or "Varies by condition".
[/list]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/h8suga/ModernRecipeBook/main/docs/screenshots/contents.png[/img]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/h8suga/ModernRecipeBook/main/docs/screenshots/chest-sources.png[/img]

[h3]And more[/h3]
[list]
[*]Coins go to the Piggy Bank first when you deposit.
[*]Stages are right for crafted items: nothing shows before its own recipe.
[*]Multiplayer storage: no items lost during Quick Stack, password-protected Magic Storage works for every player.
[*]Recent shows how many you crafted and when.
[/list]

[h2]Highlights[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Six tabs in one window[/b]: Items, Recipes, Bestiary, Storage, Recent crafts and Tracking.
[*][b]How to Get[/b] for any item: recipes, drops, bags, crates, chests, shops, fishing, shimmer and the world itself, with the drop chances of your world's difficulty and seed.
[*][b]It knows where you are in the game[/b]: every item, recipe and mob has the boss it waits for, and "Before: <boss>" shows exactly what you can get right now. "Next boss" follows your world by itself.
[*][b]Craft from everything around you[/b]: inventory, chests, banks and Magic Storage, even a network far away.
[*][b]Bestiary[/b] with animated portraits, drops, where and after which boss a mob spawns, and the stats you have earned.
[*]Sorts by damage, defense, healing, tool power and more, class buttons and weapon traits (homing, piercing, through walls...), a crafting station filter and "Craftable now".
[*]Tracking cards or compact lines under the minimap, map teleport and shared map markers.
[*]Every action has a shortcut; F1 lists them all. Back and Forward walk through everything you opened.
[/list]

[h2]Built-in Magic Storage and Calamity support[/h2]
[b]Magic Storage[/b]: every network in reach is part of the Storage tab, with its cells and its Crafting Interface stations. Craft straight from it, bring a far network in with a Portable Access, and Quick Stack, Deposit All and Restock into it, the remote one included.

[b]Calamity[/b]: every Calamity boss is a progression step (no Boss Checklist needed), Calamity's biomes and the Acid Rain tiers sort the Bestiary, spawn rules and chest loot are read from Calamity's own code, drop chances follow Revengeance and Death, and the Rogue class has its own button.

[h2]Languages[/h2]
English and Russian, including everything the book writes about items, sources and mobs. Every text is a whole phrase in localization files, so the book can be translated into any language the way Calamity is: see [url=https://github.com/h8suga/ModernRecipeBook/blob/main/TRANSLATING.md]TRANSLATING.md[/url].

[h2]На русском[/h2]
[b]Версия 1.18.0.[/b] Книга рецептов, бестиарий и обзор хранилищ для каждого предмета и моба Terraria и её модов, полностью на русском. Открывается на колёсико мыши (Mouse3). Показывает, где взять любой предмет и после какого босса он доступен, крафтит из сундуков и Magic Storage рядом (даже из удалённой сети), полностью поддерживает Calamity и Magic Storage и работает в мультиплеере.

[list]
[*]Ctrl+F из любого места игры ищет по всему сразу: по названию, по смыслу («баф», «крылья») и по описаниям, с кнопками «Создать» и «Взять» прямо в строке.
[*]Каждое имя в книге — ссылка: боссы, мобы, биомы, предметы, моды, этапы; «Назад» возвращает.
